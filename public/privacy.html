Privacy Policy for Outbox Downloader

Last Updated: November 2026

At Outbox Downloader (outboxdownloader.com), we respect your privacy. This policy outlines how information is handled when you visit our website and use our desktop client.

Key Privacy Guarantee: Zero Payload Inspection

Outbox Downloader is a native desktop client. All file transfers occur directly between your personal device and Dropbox’s edge servers via TLS 1.3. We do NOT operate proxy servers and we never store, log, inspect, or retain your files or credentials.

1. Information We Collect on the Website

When you visit our website, standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, referring page) may be recorded for performance and security purposes. If you submit a query via our contact form, we collect the name and email you provide solely to reply to your inquiry.

2. Cookies and Google AdSense

Our website displays advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on prior visits to our site and other websites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting adssettings.google.com.

3. Third-Party Links & Services

Our website and software communicate with Dropbox shared links provided by users. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party platforms or the content stored on them.

4. Contacting Us About Privacy

If you have questions or concerns about this policy, email us directly at contact@outboxdownloader.com.
